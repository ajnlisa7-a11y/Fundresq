# Fundresq Writing Rule

## Core Principle
**Write for someone who has never used a blockchain explorer.**

The visitor may have just lost money. They are distressed, cautious, and seeking clear answers. They should never be forced to decipher technical jargon before understanding whether Fundresq can help them.

Fundresq should sound like someone who has seen complicated situations before and knows how to investigate them.

---

## The Tone
**calm + precise + intelligent + reassuring**

### What It Is NOT:
- ❌ **Cyber + intimidating + overly technical** (e.g. no hacker tropes, dark telemetry buzzwords, or forensic acronym salad).
- ❌ **"We will recover your money!"** (e.g. no false guarantees, no magic recovery promises, no predatory claims).

---

## The Spouse Test
> **Could a person explain this sentence to their spouse five minutes after reading it?**  
> If not, rewrite it.

### Example:
- ❌ *“Fundresq leverages advanced multi-chain forensic intelligence to identify transactional pathways and establish asset attribution.”*
- ✅ *“We follow cryptocurrency transactions to understand where relevant funds moved.”*

Same capability. Far more trust.

---

## Guidelines

1. **Explain Technical Terminology in Ordinary English**
   Technical terms are permitted when necessary, but they must immediately be accompanied by an ordinary, everyday English explanation.
   - *Instead of:* "Cross-chain transaction tracing and attribution"
   - *Say:* "Following funds across blockchains: If funds move from one blockchain to another, we work to follow the transaction trail."
   - *Instead of:* "Counterparty clustering and peeling chain isolation"
   - *Say:* "Separating the thief's wallet transfers from unrelated network activity."
   - *Instead of:* "Evidentiary dossier with cryptographic chain-of-custody"
   - *Say:* "A clear written report with verified dates, transaction IDs, and exchange deposit accounts that police and lawyers can immediately use."

2. **Speak From the Visitor's Perspective**
   - Frame features around what happened to the person: *"Lost or stolen cryptocurrency"*, *"Sent funds to an investment platform that locked withdrawals"*, *"Drained personal wallet"*.
   - Avoid framing services purely from an academic or investigator-centric viewpoint.

3. **Zero False Promises & Zero Recovery Guarantees**
   - Explicitly warn against advance-fee recovery scammers.
   - Clearly state that Fundresq is an independent tracing and investigation practice, not a law firm or a "hack-back" recovery scheme.
   - Emphasize facts, transparency, and actionable evidence.
